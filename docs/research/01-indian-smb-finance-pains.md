# Indian SMB finance pain points (research, 2026-10-10)

Method note: reddit.com could not be fetched, so sources are news, Parliament data, court reporting,
Trustpilot/G2 reviews and practitioner forums (TechEnclave). Vendor-blog figures are marked as such.

## Ranked pains

1. **Late payments from big buyers (MSME receivables)** — ~₹7.34 lakh crore of MSME dues delayed (Mar 2024,
   GAME/FISME/C2FO). ~₹31,039 cr stuck in Samadhaan (Dec 2025); ODR portal (odr.msme.gov.in) replaced it for new
   cases from 15 Oct 2025. GST is owed on invoice date, so sellers pay tax on unpaid money. Section 43B(h) side
   effect: buyers shifting orders away from registered MSMEs.
   - Agent: reminder cadence tied to the 45/15-day MSMED clock, statutory interest calc, 43B(h) nudge, ODR filing pack.
   - Sources: storyboard18.com (delayed payments report), swarajyamag.com (government buyers), businesstoday.in (43B(h)).
2. **Freelancers chasing clients** — delays hit rent/bills; invoices held for wrong GST breakup; export realisation
   (FEMA) and LUT/IGST risk on late foreign payments.
   - Agent: GST-correct invoice check, escalation ladder, FEMA realisation tracker.
3. **Payment-gateway holds / frozen settlements** — Razorpay Trustpilot (Jul–Sep 2025): "This money blocked from 6
   months." Cashfree reviews cite unexpected freezes.
   - Agent: diagnose hold type, assemble KYC/proof-of-service pack, escalate to grievance officer / RBI.
4. **Innocent-merchant account freezes over tainted UPI receipts** (strong, underserved) — NCRP complaints freeze every
   recipient account; Kerala HC: freeze only the disputed amount. Reseller lost access to ~₹65k over a ₹14,100 receipt.
   - Agent: payer screening, per-receipt proof trail, draft representation citing the Kerala HC order.
   - Sources: thenewsminute.com, medianama.com, techenclave.com.
5. **Automated GST mismatch notices** (DRC-01B / DRC-01C; UPI-data-based notices to Bengaluru traders in 2025).
   - Agent: continuous 2B reconciliation, supplier nudges, draft replies for CA sign-off.
6. **GST compliance cost** — NIPFP (Sep 2026): 3.28–13.90% of turnover for small unincorporated firms.
7. **Reconciliation (UPI/gateway/COD → invoices)** — aliases, truncated references, net-of-fee settlements; COD
   remittance 7–15 days, 2–4% unexplained deductions (vendor figures), RTO 20–40% on COD.
   - Agent: UTR/invoice matching, courier/gateway deduction audit and claims, push to Tally/Zoho.
8. **Fake payment screenshots & "refund the overpayment" scams** — 12.64 lakh UPI fraud cases / ₹981 cr in FY25
   (Lok Sabha); 51% of victims don't complain (LocalCircles).
   - Agent: mark paid only from bank/gateway truth; block refunds to a different payer/account.
9. **Chargebacks/disputes incl. friendly fraud** — merchants win ~1/3 (PhonePe PG, unverified); tight windows.
   - Agent: auto evidence pack (POD, chats, invoice) filed before deadline.
10. **Vendor bank-detail change fraud (BEC)** — Dr Reddy's ₹2.16 cr; Bengaluru firm ₹17L; Pune insider ₹2.45 cr.
    - Agent: any beneficiary change = high risk; out-of-band verification, penny-drop name match, human approval.

## Trust in AI with money
No India-specific survey found. Salesforce: Indian SMBs' top AI concern is security, then compliance and customer
distrust. UK: 66% of finance staff worry AI reduces oversight. Design rule: agents prepare/chase/reconcile autonomously;
money-out actions (payouts, refunds, bank changes) need policy limits and human approval.

## Most underserved
1. Innocent-merchant UPI account freezes.
2. Statutory dues enforcement (MSMED interest + 43B(h) + automated ODR filing).
3. D2C COD/courier/gateway deduction audits + reconciliation, bundled with refund/payout fraud controls.
